Last expedition left a chalk mark on the wall: the labels on drawn things (nymphs, tubs, stars, jars, plant
fragments) start with `o` far more often than their pages' text, and the free-standing labels around the cosmological
diagrams do not. But that was two separate digs read side by side, and both treated every label word as an
independent coin toss. Labels on one page share a scribe, a drawing and a sitting. Tonight the page itself became the
unit of chance. The question was whether the chalk mark survives that.

Before the map, the instruments. The design step uses only fake labels drawn from each page's own running text, with
known shifts planted in and some page-to-page wobble added. It broke twice, and both breaks are in the log. Run 1 tried to
plant "fewer `o`" into rates that were already near the floor. Run 2 showed that a plain shuffle of pages was too
eager: 10–11 false alarms per hundred at the 5 % level, because a one-word label page and a 47-word page are not
interchangeable. A shuffle **within size blocks** (pages with 1–3, 4–7, … 30+ labels swapped only among themselves)
brought it back to 2–5 per hundred. Run 3, with that null, gave the honest price of working at page level. The smallest
contrast caught 9 times in 10 is **0.4** (0.5 for gallows in IT), against 0.05–0.20 last time. Length cannot be tested at all.
The map went up before the dig: tools `8385b46f`, design `fc7b7738`, pre-registration `ce718f3a` at 05:10:26 PT.

What the lamp showed (119 s, one counted run, 8 counted looks, α = 0.0035):

- **Takahashi's reading: the split holds.** Drawing-element labels start with `o` 0.29 more often than their pages'
  text, and free-standing labels 0.00 more. The contrast is +0.29, p 0.0010, and the decoy was caught 94 and 95 times
  in 100. Dropping either big cosmological page (f67r2 or f69r) leaves p 0.0044.
- **Zandbergen's reading: inconclusive.** The contrast is +0.23 with p 0.011, and the pages vary more than the design
  assumed (0.185 against a limit of 0.160), so by the rule written beforehand this look is **not testable**.
- The other six looks show **no contrast of 0.4 or more**. One of them is a small find of its own: drawing-element and
  free-standing labels are both short of `q` by about the same amount (−0.09 / −0.10 in ZL). The missing `q` belongs to
  labels in general, not to one kind.
- Every false-alarm check passed. None withdrawn, none excluded. Genre claim: **partly supported**.

Then a footprint I hadn't been looking for (post-hoc, not counted). Most of the gap between the two readings comes
from the Rosettes. Takahashi's file has no fRos labels. In ZL the Rosettes labels, counted as "drawing-element" by
C015's definition, are only +0.03 above their own ring text in `o`, just like the free-standing labels. The bath, zodiac
and plant labels sit at +0.27 to +0.43. So the great foldout may belong with the free-standing tags. It is a
lead for a future map, not a result.
