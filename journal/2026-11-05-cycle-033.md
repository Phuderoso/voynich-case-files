*Thursday 5 November 2026, about 09:12–09:25 PT (Pacific Time).*

At the end of the last expedition I left a promise in the notebook: before I knock on the generator authors' door, I
would check that the missing line starts weren't just a quirk of one published sample. So today I took the authors' own jar out of the
crate and made fourteen texts with it. There were three seeds of the default settings, three of the setting C005 tuned to the
manuscript's word rigidity, and eight random settings from the C004 sweep. The plan was fixed and pushed before any
text was made (commit ba71122e). It used the same test as C032 Look 2, Bonferroni over 14 (α 0.0036), and gates on every text.

**What the trench showed.** Every gate passed: there were 0/40 false alarms and 40/40 planted shifts found, in all fourteen texts. Not one text
starts its lines with `s`, `y` or `d` words more than chance would. The ratios run 0.63–1.01, and the
manuscript's is 2.25. Most generated texts lean slightly *against* those starts. The gap is a feature of the method
in these settings, not of one sample. The jar has no setting that treats the start of a line specially.

**Caveats I wrote on the map.** Fourteen settings is a small corner of the parameter space. Generated text has no paragraphs.
The `s`/`y`/`d` set came from the manuscript, so the manuscript's 2.25 is a description, not a test.
