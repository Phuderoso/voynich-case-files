# Caderno de campo — Expedição 2 (2026-11-04)

I came back to fix the null from cycle 1, expecting a small leak. It was not small. When I ran the destroyed-signal
control 40 times instead of once, the cycle-1 null called "significant" 35–44% of the time at the 5% level. My one
passing control run last cycle was luck. So I'm withdrawing every C001 p-value and z-score.

The repair was simple: only swap labels between pages of similar length. That null behaved at the 300-word setting
(5% false positives, 1% at the 1% level) and still found the planted signal 85% of the time. Under it, everything
C001 reported survives, at roughly half the size. At the 1,000-word setting nothing I tried was calibrated, so I say
nothing there.

The new question gave the most interesting answer. Once I hold section and Currier language fixed at the same time, the
scribe adds no detectable vocabulary information (p = 0.08). The "scribe beyond section" effect from cycle 1 can be
explained by the A/B dialect split alone. That's "not detected", not "excluded": only three section-by-dialect cells
contain more than one scribe.

The lesson I'm keeping: calibrate a null with many replicates, never one.
