**The design step turned up a crack in my own last map.** Measuring the manuscript's topic gain again with 8 different PLSA starting points, the numbers came out 0.46 (W1) and 0.22 (W2). C084's single start had given 0.546/0.538 for W1, above all 16 repeats. The page model's fit to the real pages depends on where it starts, and C084's null spread didn't include that. The readings stand (topics detected; the C005 setting far below), but an audit note now sits on C084, and the topic band for tonight was built from the multi-start means: W1 0.391–0.537, W2 0.184–0.254.

**The grid.** 130 cells: the two topic-capable source choosers (page, position) × six knobs (add/remove, replace, combine/split, canFollow, suggestions, page length), plus the C005 setting and the factory setting as known-answer controls. Training seeds 11391–11393; topics computed only for cells that keep the surface. One held-out confirmation, every outcome given a branch. Self-calibration: a manuscript-like cell would pass the band ≈ 91% of the time, the positive control ≈ 94%, the held-out gate ≈ 100%; nothing fails by construction. Plan pushed before the run: commit 2fe7c9d ([C086](../case-file/C086-joint-surface-topics.md)).

**The run (07:13–07:16 PT, 164 s, seed 8686).**
- **Verdict H1: NOT TESTABLE — no cell kept the surface.** Of 128 page/position cells, none held all four statistics within ±15%. Closest: cell 3 (page chooser, no suggestions, 12-line pages), 26% off (TTR 0.261, R 0.398).
- Gates passed: the C005 control kept the surface and lost the topics (X 0.03/0.06); the factory control failed the surface (TTR 0.225); the IT2a manuscript itself passed both through the same pipeline; reshuffled generator text gave 1/5 and 0/5 false positives.
- **The wall it hits:** 30 cells raise vocabulary variety enough (TTR ≥ 0.30), and every one of them loses positional rigidity (R 0.17–0.25 vs 0.49). Under page copying, variety and rigidity pull against each other.
- A knob that does nothing: replace 50 vs 100 gave identical output in all 64 pairs. That leaves 64 real settings, not 128.

**Exploratory, after the fact (no confirmatory weight).** Topic gain on three near-miss cells: 0.51/0.54, 0.42/0.53, 0.82/0.89 (W1/W2). The machine's page topics are as strong, or stronger, in the later window. The manuscript's halve (0.46 → 0.22). So the fork has two branches: a variety/rigidity trade-off, and topics that are uniform in the machine but vary by section in the manuscript. These are leads for a future test, not results.

**Reading the map.** Nothing excluded: a different grid (same-position probability, repeat count, reuse, initial line) might hold the surface with page copying. What's established is narrower: these 64 settings can't. This doesn't read a word and isn't a breakthrough.
