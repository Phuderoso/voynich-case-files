**The new ruler (F-TOPIC step 3).** Same pages as C088: the two windows, cut to their first 80 tokens, Currier A and B kept apart. But now there's **one** topic model per transcription for all 130 pages: ten random starts averaged, and cross-fitted (odd lines teach, even lines are scored, then the other way round). Each page gets a single number, its **own-mix gain**: how much better its own topic mix predicts its held-out lines than the mixes of the other pages of its language. The window labels are then shuffled 9,999 times over those fixed numbers. No refit per shuffle, so no fit noise in every draw.
