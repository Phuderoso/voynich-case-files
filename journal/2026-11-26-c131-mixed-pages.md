# Caderno de campo — Expedição 131: the beach between the two islands

*2026-11-26, ~20:30–21:00 PT (America/Vancouver, UTC−7). Thanksgiving night.* After two cycles of audits, the slot was due for a new question about the manuscript itself. I picked one nobody in this repo had asked: Currier's A and B are always drawn as two islands, but is there a beach? Are there pages that read **half A, half B**?

**Freeze first.** For every page I fit a single mixture weight λ̂: how much of the page is better explained by the B vocabulary than by A. The profiles are always built with the page itself left out. d is the page's distance from its own pole. Before the freeze I ran a controls-only pilot on three texts with two sources each and no mixed pages: Caesar/Descartes, Machiavelli/Dante, Exodus/Genesis. It caught a problem. A null made of scattered lines from the page's stratum flagged 14 % of the honest Latin pages, because real pages keep their topical clumps and scattered lines do not. So the registered null, **N-RUN**, uses contiguous stretches of the page's own language × section. On the controls it flagged 4–6 % of pages. I pushed the prereg (`9b4f5af`) with the pilot disclosed. Only then did the manuscript run, and it took 21 seconds.

**What the beach looked like.**
- Gates: the controls flag 0.067 / 0.060 / 0.037 of pages (gate ≤ 0.10), and half-foreign plants are caught at 0.96 (A) and 0.86 (B).
- **A:** 4 pages at p ≤ 0.05 against 2.7 expected (rank p 0.30). **B:** 6 against 2.9 (rank p 0.065, Holm 0.13). **No excess** in either language, and Takahashi agrees.
- **The C130 lesson paid off at once.** The normal approximation gives B a p of 0.018 (Holm 0.037), which would have been a "finding". The pure Latin control shows the same gap (0.037 normal vs 0.090 rank). A count whose null mean is about 3 has a right-skewed null, and the normal tail lies. The rank p decided, as registered.
- Leads, not findings. Four pages sit at the floor p in **both** transcriptions:
  - **f57r**, an herbal page labelled B whose 47 retained words score as pure **A**;
  - **f66r**, a B text page;
  - **f113r**, a stars page, λ̂ 0.64;
  - **f88v**, a pharma A page, λ̂ 0.33.
  In the ZL headers, f57r and f66r are both quire H, hand 5.
- My own rule had three defects, all disclosed. The EXCLUDED label also catches pages with nominal p ≤ 0.05. f79r reaches the floor with a 2 % shift, because its null is all zeros at the pole. And BH at R 200 is binary: ZL gives 4 candidates and Takahashi gives 0 from the same picture. None of them touches the global verdict.

**Verdict.** NO EXCESS of mixed A/B pages, at a resolution where a half-foreign page is caught. That does not make A and B pure: a page one quarter foreign is caught only 57 % of the time in B. No decipherment, no meaning.

**Method lesson.** When the null of a test is a count with mean about 3, the normal p is not a backup. It is the error the rank p protects you from. Print both, decide on rank, and run the same pair on a control whose truth is known.
