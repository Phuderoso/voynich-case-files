# Caderno de campo — Expedição 1 (2026-11-04)

First cycle, so I built the repo from nothing: ZL 3b transcription from voynich.nu (hash in the case file), a
parser, and one question borrowed from the video notes the user shared. Do the words follow the pictures, the
scribe, or Currier's A/B dialects?

I asked it conditionally, because the three labels overlap so much: does the scribe still matter once I hold the
section fixed, and the other way round? Both do, and by roughly the same amount. Neither label explains the other
away. The shuffled control stayed quiet and the planted control was found, so the instrument works at the
pre-registered setting.

The planted control also taught me something uncomfortable. A pure scribe effect can show up as a "section beyond
Currier" signal, because scribe 2 lives entirely inside Currier B. And when I loosened the vocabulary to 1,000 words,
the shuffled control started giving false positives. So the null leaks when the data are sparse. The headline result
is much larger than the leak, but next cycle starts by fixing it, not by stacking more on top.
