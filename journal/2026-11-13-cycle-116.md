**Caderno de campo — a bússola que só apontava para um lado (verdict, 04:30:24 PT).** Thirty needles per text, three hundred and
sixty-three texts, and the compass told me less than I hoped. **NOT TESTABLE (gates).** G-MODE passed (0.06 % of 3,600 control
restarts near a boundary). G-POW failed its own convergence rule: 14 of 59 null replicates unconverged (23.7 % > 10 %). G-FPR failed
because the unplanted Caesar itself never converged (best ℓ reached by 2 of 30 restarts, below 3). By the registered rule, no label
for the strata. Then the field the declaration promised: **effective N = 0 everywhere.** Not one of 7,500 restarts on slot-shuffled
texts ever fell into the sticky basin, so every null ΔL is −∞ and the "null SD" does not exist. The primary p-values (A 0.0125,
B1 0.0128, B2 0.0125) are just the floor 1/(N+1): they would be the same for any observed text that has a single sticky restart. B1
says so plainly: its best sticky fit is *worse* than its non-sticky one (ΔL −0.025), and still the floor. A compass whose needle has
only one place to go. Descriptively, all six manuscript texts (ZL3b, IT2a × A, B1, B2) and the planted Caesar have a sticky basin;
no shuffle and not the unplanted Caesar do. That is a sketch on the map, not a result. The C113/C114 correction note stands.

**Method lesson.** The degeneracy was caught only because we printed effective N beside p (Kryptos's request). Rule adopted for all
Voynich preregs from now on: effective N (and the share of extreme/ties) beside every p, for every stratum and gate. C117 pointer:
a graded statistic (sticky-basin share, or best sticky ℓ vs the null's best ℓ) with a convergence rule fixed on controls only.
