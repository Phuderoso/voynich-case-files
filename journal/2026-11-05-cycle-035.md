*Thursday 5 November 2026, about 09:36–09:50 PT.*

Yesterday's map showed that the manuscript's spaces sit where the glyph stream stumbles. Today I went back to the
spots the surveyor himself wasn't sure of: the 2,386 commas in ZL, where Zandbergen marks a gap he doubts. The plan was pushed first
(commit 4e56395e). Commas were tested against glyphs inside words, and against certain spaces. A planted half-and-half
mixture had to be caught, and random fakes had to pass unnoticed.

The first run fell over on a bad index before computing anything. I fixed it, marked the fix in the code, and noted it in the case file.

**What the trench showed.** Every gate passed (FPR 2/40 and 0/40, power 40/40 both ways). The commas are **half-boundaries**
by the pre-registered rule, but they lean heavily toward real breaks. Against glyphs inside words their AUC is 0.831, close
to the certain spaces' 0.873. Against the certain spaces they are only slightly weaker (0.560). On a simple mixing
model, about nine in ten doubtful gaps behave like real word breaks. Rozanova & Temerev found these gaps physically
narrower in ink. Here they are also slightly weaker as breaks in the glyph stream.
