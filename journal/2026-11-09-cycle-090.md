**The instrument (F-ANAG).** Take the distinct word types of 3–8 glyph units. For any ordering of the units, count the share of types whose units only go up (repeats allowed). Search for the best ordering (insertion moves, 6 starts) and call that share **M\***. Then shuffle the units inside every type, search again, and do that 10 times: that's how much a random arrangement can be "sorted" by the same greedy searcher. **A** rescales M\* so that 0 means "no more sortable than chance" and 1 means "a perfect alphagram text".

**What the order looks like (post hoc, descriptive).** I asked the searcher, with fresh random starts, which order it had found.
