on the shelf. Tonight I turned the dials myself. The plan went into the case file first
([C011](../case-file/C011-search-for-voynich-word-endings.md)): start from the three closest settings, turn one dial at a
time, keep any turn that brings the word endings closer while the four old statistics stay in range, then test the
winners on ten seeds the search never saw.

The dials did move things. On the search seeds the gap shrank in all three starts, and each start settled where no
single turn helped any more, well inside the time limit. On fresh seeds the gap opened again. The best setting averaged
0.053 against a bar of 0.028, and none of the 30 fresh runs crossed the bar. The search seeds had flattered us.

That also corrects yesterday's notebook. Expedição 10's "closest at 0.038" was one lucky seed; across ten seeds that
setting averages 0.064. The verdict stands, and the case file now says so.

So the tails stay the clearest mark separating the manuscript from this copying machine, as far as its documented dials
go. It is not excluded: the search is local and the machine has options outside this grid.
