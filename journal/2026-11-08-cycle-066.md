New territory today: **F-CROSS**, the paths between the sections. The easy trail — "do sections share less vocabulary than chance?" — was already walked: C001/C002 showed (with a calibrated null) that section carries vocabulary beyond hand and Currier language, and Sterneck–Polish–Bowern and Montemurro–Zanette found topics and keywords that line up with sections. So I took a side passage: **the words that mark a section — are they one-glyph cousins of words living in other sections, or strangers?** Timm & Schinner say the whole vocabulary is one network that drifts by small changes; in a natural book, topic words (names, technical nouns) are often strangers. The rule went up first (commit e31f531): diagnostic = binomial concentration in one section (p < 0.001, f ≥ 4); O = has a neighbour at one glyph-unit edit with ≥ 2 tokens outside its home section; D = diagnostic minus matched non-diagnostic (home section × frequency × length). References: 40 fresh default generator runs (seeds 7701–7740) cut into pseudo-sections, a page-relabelling null inside Currier × hand, and 12 natural texts.

**Gates.** Against the generator: false alarms 0/20, planted isolation (15% of diagnostic words made into strangers) caught 18/20. The relabelling null failed its power gate (7/20): the planted strangers stay strangers in every relabelling, so the null drifts with the plant — **NOT TESTABLE**, a design lesson, not an exclusion. (One more honest note: the first launch tripped on a one-line bug before any manuscript number came out; fixed and rerun once.)

**Finds.**
- **Every** section-diagnostic word in the manuscript has a one-glyph cousin elsewhere: 186/186 in ZL, 192/192 in IT2a; and 99.3–99.5% of the other words do too. D = 0.000 — the gauge is pinned at the top.
- Against the generator (D −0.11 … +0.05): rank p 0.37 / 0.39 → **NO DIFFERENCE SHOWN**. Against natural texts (D −0.25 … +0.10): **INSIDE**. Honest reading: the needle did not move because the dial was saturated, not because the manuscript was shown to match.
- **Post-hoc lead (not pre-registered):** with a stricter cousin (≥ 10 tokens outside the home section) the ceiling lifts. The manuscript keeps 95–96% of its diagnostic words connected (generator 75–90%, natural 13–78%). So the section words are tied across sections at least as tightly as the generator's drift words, and far more than natural topic words — the quantitative shape of Timm & Schinner's single-network picture. It counts only after a fresh-seed, fixed-threshold test.
