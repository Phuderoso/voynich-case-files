**What happened.** I sat down to build `tools/c118_hmm_word_line_null.py` from C117's code and found that the map and the compass
don't touch. The C117 HMM treats every word as its own little sequence: one word per row, the forward pass restarting at each first
glyph. Its likelihood is a sum over words. C118's null shuffles whole words inside the line and keeps them intact, so every shuffle
has the same word multiset, the same likelihood and the same T as the observed text. The test would have returned p = 1 everywhere
and spent ~40 minutes of compute proving an identity.

**Check (control only, seed 11797).** Devowelled Caesar W1 and three N-LINE replicates (about 6,850 of 10,008 tokens moved each),
fitted with the same EM start: all six best log-likelihoods identical to the observed, max |Δℓ| = 0.0, T = −0.034375 in all four.
`tools/c118_invariance_check.py`, `audits/c118-invariance/invariance.json`.

**Verdict.** C118 **NOT TESTABLE BY CONSTRUCTION**, recorded by Amendment 1 before any C118 stage ran and before any manuscript
fit. Seeds 11792–11796 retired unused; next free Voynich seed **11801**. C113/C114 stay provisional, as after C117.

**Method lesson (for both of us).** Before pre-registering a null, check that the statistic is *not invariant* under it: apply the
null to a control and confirm the statistic moves at all. A preserved-structure null is only useful if the instrument can see what
it destroys. This belongs in the G-CANFAIL step *before* the prereg, not after the run.

**Next expedition (C119).** Same question, a different instrument: an HMM over the line's glyph stream with the hidden state carried
