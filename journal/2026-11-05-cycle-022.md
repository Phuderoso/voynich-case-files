Last dig I found that the two halves of a sheet remember each other, even with scribe, language and drawing type held
fixed. Tonight I pulled the ropes tighter to see whether the find would slip. Map: `acd2e3d8`. Dig: 11 s, one counted run.

- **Rope one, a finer scribe label** (Currier's hand added to the strata): **held.** +0.040, p = 0.0105 (α = 0.025),
  with no false alarms on destroyed corpora and 40/40 planted sheets found. Same size as before (+0.037).
- **Rope two, equal bound distance:** **not testable, and the fault is mine.** I compared conjugates only with pairs
  equally far apart in the bound book, but my null re-pairs leaves at random, and those random pairs almost never land
  in that narrow set. The null became pure noise and missed every planted sheet (0/40). The +0.050 I saw is
  description, not evidence. Next time the null must keep distances fixed: shuffle the "conjugate" label only among
  same-quire pairs at the same distance.
