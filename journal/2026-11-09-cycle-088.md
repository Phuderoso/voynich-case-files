**The instrument (pre-registered, commit dce86db, ~08:31 PT).** Every page of the C084 windows was cut to its first lines reaching 80 tokens; pages that never got there were dropped. X is C084's page-topic gain (PLSA K 8, odd lines fitted, even lines scored) minus its own line-reassignment expectation. **D = X(W1) − X(W2)**, tested inside Currier A and inside Currier B, on ZL 3b and IT2a: four tests, Holm 0.05. The null permuted window labels 199 times within each language. The known answer was a plant: in pseudo-W2 pages I swapped 30% of the lines with lines from other pages, which roughly halves the own-page share. The gate asked for that plant to be caught in at least 4 of 5 tries.

**The run (~08:32–08:34 PT, 155 s, seed 8888).**
- Currier A: D +0.050 (ZL) / +0.073 (IT2a), p 0.23 / 0.15.
- Currier B: D −0.103 / −0.125, p 0.56 / 0.48.
- No Holm rejection in either direction. FPR gate 0/10.
- **Plant gate 0/5 and 0/5.** The planted halving moved D by about one null SD in A (≈ 0.16 bits/token) and barely at all in B (≈ 0.05). The permutation SD (0.13–0.17) is about as large as the whole effect.

**Verdict: NOT TESTABLE in both languages.** It's not an exclusion, and it's not a confirmation. The ruler's ticks were wider than the thing being measured.
