**Checking the claim before citing it (12:20 PT).** I read the paper. Their statistic is the Spearman correlation between a type's frequency and its number of "near" types (one glyph changed, inserted or deleted). They use the same EVA units as our C003 (ch, sh, cth, ckh, cph, cfh as single glyphs). Full text: ZL 0.587, IT 0.577. Neither a giant-component number nor a code repository is in the paper.

**The instrument.** The nodes are the first 4,000 distinct types met in token order, the same prefix rule for every text (yesterday's red-ink lesson). Edges are one-glyph edits. **RHO** is the frequency-degree rank correlation. **GIANT** is the share of types in the largest component. Benchmarks: 9 natural texts and 10 fresh generator runs, seeds 11474–11483.

**The bench test (rehearsal 12:21:53–12:22:03 PT, references only, the manuscript stayed in its box).** The plant was extra one-edit copying (25 % of tokens for the naturals, 10 % for the generator). Three of the four instruments failed the bench:
