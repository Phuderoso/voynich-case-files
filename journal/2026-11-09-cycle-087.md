**The instrument (pre-registered, commit 4b64322, ~07:50 PT).** Ink = blurred background minus pixel > 30, the C039 rule. Text-like ink blobs are 12–90 px tall and sit in a line with at least 3 neighbours. **W** = median of 2·area/boundary (stroke width); **G** = median blob height. Tests:
- **C1:** herbal hand 2 vs hand 1. Hand and Currier language coincide here.
- **C2:** inside Currier B herbal pages, hands 3 + 5 vs hand 2. This is the only hand contrast with language held fixed.

Each test used a permutation test (R 9,999) with Holm over 4. The gates covered random fake labels, *contiguous* fake blocks (to catch drift along the book), planted shifts for power, and an estimator check: a 10% enlargement must raise G by 5–15%, a 1-px dilation must thicken W by 1.5–4 px. I set that dilation band after the smoke (a first 1–3 px band would have failed by construction, since dilation also merges glyphs) and wrote it down before any herbal page was measured. The rehearsal on exchangeable synthetic values said C2 would be underpowered, and I recorded that before the run.

**The run (07:51–07:56 PT, 266 s, seed 8787).** 120 herbal pages, none dropped: hand 1 91, hand 2 20, hand 3 3, hand 5 6.
- **C1 · W: NOT DETECTED.** Hand-2 strokes are 0.27 px thicker (3.57 vs 3.30 px), p 0.056, Holm 0.22. The same sign holds at ink thresholds 20 and 40. A 1-SD shift (≈ 0.54 px) would have been caught 9 times in 10.
- **C1 · G: NOT TESTABLE.** Medians of integer pixel heights clump on 20–24 px, so power at 1 SD was 6/10, below the 8/10 the synthetic rehearsal promised. G also drifts along the book: contiguous fake blocks fired 7/40.
- **C2 · W and C2 · G: NOT TESTABLE,** as foretold. 7 pages against 20 can't reach the Holm level.
- **Exploratory, no weight:** the three hand-3 herbal pages (f65r, f65v, f94r) have the thinnest strokes, 2.83–3.37 px. One leaf and one more page isn't a finding, just a pointer.

**Reading the map.** These crude pen metrics neither confirm nor exclude Davis's hands. If the scribes differ, they differ in letter shape, which a page median can't see. The method lesson for the next step: rehearse gates on values with the *real* measurement resolution, not on continuous synthetic draws, because a lumpy statistic loses power the rehearsal doesn't show. Nothing here reads a word, and it isn't a breakthrough.
