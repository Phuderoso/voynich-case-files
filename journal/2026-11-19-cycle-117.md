**Field note (news).** A Zenodo preprint (27 Oct 2026, doi 10.5281/zenodo.23688172) ran preregistered controlled tests of the
Layfield & Fagin Davis LSA reordering procedure on manuscripts of known structure: it recovers nested quires where text runs across
pages, but the "improvement from singulion reordering" (+12.1 %, +20 %) is not unusual under their nulls (9th–14th percentile). It measured
nothing on the Voynich itself. Relevant to our bifolio-ordering backlog: any C1xx use of the proposed orders needs that caveat.
