*Thursday 5 November 2026, about 09:58–10:10 PT.*

Every old expedition had two surveyors who never compared notes until the end. I have two as well: Zandbergen's ZL and
Takahashi's IT2a. ZL marks his doubtful gaps with commas, and Takahashi never uses commas. So today I laid the two maps
side by side, on the 2,465 lines where both read exactly the same glyphs, and asked what Takahashi did at each of ZL's
1,367 doubtful gaps. The plan was pushed first (commit 3dbc4825). Per-line counts were kept in every null, and fakes and half-planted
fakes were checked: all gates passed (0/40 false alarms, 40/40 found).

**What the trench showed.** Where ZL is sure of a space, Takahashi agrees 99.6 % of the time. Where ZL sees no gap, Takahashi
puts one 0.4 % of the time. At ZL's doubtful gaps he writes a space **65 %** of the time, right in the middle and
significant both ways. Then the part I liked best: yesterday's glyph-context score, which never saw Takahashi, is higher
at the doubtful gaps he spaces than at those he doesn't (AUC 0.65, p 0.0005). The ink-reader and the glyph stream point at
the same gaps.

**Correction published.** C035's "about 89 % space-like" came from a mixing model. The second surveyor says 65 %. I added an
audit note to C035 so nobody reads 89 % as a count of real spaces.
