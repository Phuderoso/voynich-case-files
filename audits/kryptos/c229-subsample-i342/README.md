## EN
- **Design:** whole-cell draws inside family × nq-window pools (windows 10–12 … 31–40; levels N 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000; cells cannot be split), fixed-effect slopes, cluster bootstrap over the 16 nulls, 4 tests, Holm 0.05, "distance to the bar" labels (`robusto` / `FRONTEIRA`), Wilson 95% beside rates.
- **Result:** **T1 MATERIAL**, search-size slope at fixed nq and family b_A = **+0.190 NC per e-fold of N** (95% CI 0.182–0.197; +0.44 per decade; 8.8× the 0.05/decade materiality unit; p 0.002 at the bootstrap floor; robusto). **T2 MATERIAL**, length slope at fixed N −c_B = **0.0171 per quadgram** (0.0154–0.0185; 1.7× the 0.01 unit; robusto). **T3 INCONCLUSIVE** (composition effect on b 0.0094 = 5% of b_A, p 0.112) and **T4 INCONCLUSIVE** (on c 0.0023 = 13% of c_B, p 0.883): ignoring family changes neither coefficient enough to reject at the declared margins. So the I-342 bar needs both terms inside the covered range (per-draw N 10–2000); family composition is not what separates them here. Descriptive: bank_Q_r10 is the outlier family (b 0.164, c −0.0094).
- **Caveats:** replicas are a fresh-seed rerun and two disjoint halves of the same 16 nulls (no fresh nulls); the slopes (b 0.19, c −0.017) are not the c229 pooled partials (+0.056, −0.024): different estimand, and the post hoc level-to-level slope falls only slowly (0.21 → 0.17 over N 10 → 1000); nothing here covers N of 10^5–10^6. No bar is proposed; not a decipherment.
