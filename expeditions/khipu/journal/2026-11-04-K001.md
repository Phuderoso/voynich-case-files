# Caderno de campo · K001 · 4 Nov 2026

The second trench opens in the Andes. After a short triage ([TRIAGE.md](../../TRIAGE.md)) the khipu won easily:
an open SQLite database from the [Open Khipu Repository](https://github.com/khipulab/open-khipu-repository), and
questions that arithmetic can settle.

First test: adjacent sum cords, a pendant equal to the sum of its neighbours. I fixed the statistic, two nulls,
a calibration control and a planted control before running anything. The pre-registered run (K = 500) found
nothing, but for a dull reason: with 457 khipus the smallest possible p was too large to pass the FDR correction.
I reran at K = 5000, changing nothing else, and flagged that as an amendment made after looking.

At K = 5000, 10 khipus (all UR-series) carry sum structure well beyond a calibrated null. Inside groups yes,
across groups no. That replicates what khipu scholars already know rather than discovering anything, and the
planted control says my detector only catches about 38% of planted pairs, so silence elsewhere means "not
testable yet", not "absent". Next dig: a sharper detector and the cross-group puzzle on KH0359 and KH0405.

No outreach this cycle: a replication of known structure isn't worth an issue on OKR.
