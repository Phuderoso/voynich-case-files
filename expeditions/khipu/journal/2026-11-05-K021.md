07:45 PT. After so many days counting knots, today I looked at the dye. The question from open line 6 was simple:
does the khipu-keeper tell you which cord is a total by its colour?

I wrote two guesses down before looking. First, that the total stands out from its neighbours like a red flag. It
doesn't: with full power to see it, the totals are no more colour-isolated than any other cord. That idea goes into
the closed-trenches box. Second, that the totals share one colour among themselves. They do, strongly: in UR267A all
16 totals are one colour, in UR269 16 of 17.

Then I distrusted my own luck. Totals live in the same groups, and groups are often one colour. So I re-ran it with
the totals shuffled only inside their own group (labelled as a post-hoc check). Most of the effect still holds,
carried by three Inkawasi khipus: UR267A, UR269 and UR275.

And, as a good explorer checks the old maps, the Khipu Field Guide already marks "coloured pendant sums" on these
Inkawasi khipus. So no new temple, but now there's a calibrated measurement of something people had only described.
Details in [K021](../case-file/K021-sum-cord-colour.md).
