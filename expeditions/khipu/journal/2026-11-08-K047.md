- **Termination: not readable.** Paris·Lima 83/87 against a chance level of 81.14 (p 0.047), but

  copy-all control itself failed the Bonferroni bar (p 0.034 > 0.00833) because with margins 36+1,

- **Knot direction: two degenerate, one not readable.** Pereyra wrote `Z` on every usable knot of
