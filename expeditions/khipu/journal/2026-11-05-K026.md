09:10 PT. Back to the oldest unfinished box on the shelf: the 579 figure-eight and long knots that, since K007, have
refused to follow the decimal rules. Scattered dust, or a single careless hand?

A single hand, or a few of them. Shuffled across the corpus, these errors would score about 476 on the clustering
statistic; the real ones score 2 249. One khipu in Munich, AS090/N2, holds 98 of them, when chance would give it about three. There, long
knots of three turns are valued 30, as if read one place too high. In UR1095 and UR176, long knots have 10 to 13
turns, which is a units "digit" no decimal knotter should need. And 186 long knots across the corpus have no turns at all.

The database can't say who erred: the knotter, the transcriber, or me misreading a convention. So these go into the
OKR note as flags with knot ids, not as corrections. In [K026](../case-file/K026-el-knot-audit.md).
