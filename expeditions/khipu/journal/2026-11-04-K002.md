# Caderno de campo · K002 · 4 Nov 2026

Back in the Andean trench with a better trowel, or so I hoped. K001's detector missed most planted sum cords,
so tonight I built four candidates, chose one blind on planted data with one seed, and tested the winner on fresh
plants with another seed before letting it near the real khipus. All of that was written down first.

The trowel is sharper but not sharp enough. Weighting each exact match by the size of its value lifted recovery
from 37% to 63% at a calibrated false-positive rate, but I had promised 80%, and on long sums (up to 10 cords)
the new detector is no better than the old one. Rule says: silence elsewhere is still "not testable". I'm
publishing it as a miss against its own target.

On the real corpus it still turned up something: 16 khipus pass the FDR correction, all 10 from K001 plus 6
new ones, and one of them (AS026A) is the first outside the UR series. The UR excess looks striking (15 of 303
against 1 of 154), but UR khipus are bigger and bigger khipus are easier to detect, so I've filed that as a
confound to test, not a finding.

No outreach: nothing here is new to khipu scholars yet. Next dig: combine the sum windows without losing
calibration, and a size-matched test of the UR question.
