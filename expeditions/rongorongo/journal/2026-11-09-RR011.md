Keiti, tablet E, is a burned plank with one tune in its head: `040 300.028x 004.430 022.380y`, again and again. rochal
asks whether that is a refrain, a line sung at the end of each verse. Verses keep time, so I wrote the rule before
digging: take every sign pair that comes back four or more times, measure the gaps between its returns, and ask whether
they are steadier than the same lines give with their blocks reshuffled (each line keeps its length, its signs and
every refrain intact; only the positions move).

**Line 2: NOT TESTABLE.** The trench is too shallow. Keiti has six such refrains with only 27 gaps between them. I
buried a perfectly periodic refrain in shuffled copies of the tablet to see if the test could find it: T1 found it 32
times in 100, T2 only 10 (the gate is 80). For the record, as description only: the real gaps are, if anything, *less*
regular than chance (T1 0.955 vs 0.81, p 0.92); the phase statistic sits at p 0.040, 0.080 after Holm. A second script
in numpy recomputed both to the ninth decimal. One thing stood out on the map, and I mark it as post hoc:
`040 300.028x` returns four times at 13, 14, 14 and 15 signs in the first three lines, then vanishes for 138. That looks
like a short run of verses, not a song that lasts the whole tablet.

**Fallback, line 4d: NOT TESTABLE, but it leans.** RR010's loose strokes: does the stroke that stepped out of the sign
stand on the same side it was fused on? Six informative cases, and all six keep the side. The null uses each stroke's
side habit on the 23 objects other than H, P and Q, and it expects 3.49. Exact p = 0.0101. But that is also the floor,
because only six out of six could ever reject, and the power under a 95% copying rule is 0.735, just under the 0.80 gate.
So the verdict stays NOT TESTABLE, as the rule says. Two of the six cut against habit: `009` before `244` and `003`
before `254` sit on their rarer side, and the loose stroke follows the copy anyway. That is what "the same stroke,
moved out" predicts. It is a lead, and these are the cases that suggested it.
