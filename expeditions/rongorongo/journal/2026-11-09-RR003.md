## What the ground gave back

- The entry-initial count is low rather than high. Checked afterwards, the lower tail is p 0.046, and 0.091 once the
  change of direction is paid for. It goes in the notebook as a lead: strokes may avoid the slot just after a delimiter.
- Taken with RR002: on these data the free strokes show no established order, no strong clumping and no entry-final
  habit. The affix behaviour rochal sees lives in the *fused* strokes, and that is the next line (line 4, new).
