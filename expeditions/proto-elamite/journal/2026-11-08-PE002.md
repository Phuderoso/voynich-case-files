PE001 left a clear debt: decide the power *before* the test. And the 209 multi-line reverses turned out to be a
smaller trench than they looked: under PE001's whole-tablet damage rule only 8 survive. So I changed the unit of the dig
from the tablet to the line: every intact line counts, damaged ones are just missing. That gave 145 tablets and
176–408 testable reverse lines per number system.

The rule went in first (`012c5f9`): for each system S, D, B, C, does a reverse line equal the sum of a contiguous run
(*run*) or of any set (*subset*, small tablets only) of ≥ 2 obverse entries? Null: reverse lines shuffled among
tablets inside value quintiles, so "big tablet, big numbers" can't pass for arithmetic. Two stages: first the false
alarm rate and a power curve from planted sums, saved and committed (`1b2d623`) before I looked at a single real count;
then the test, BH over whatever passed.

Stage 1 caught a flaw in my own design: in S, D and B the *run* test raised false alarms 15–18 % of the time. The
rank strata split equal values by line position, and that breaks the shuffle's symmetry (confirmed afterwards: with
tie-aware strata it falls to ~4 %). Those three tests are **NOT TESTABLE**, as written. The other five were powered (80 %
power already at 10–20 % planted sums).

Stage 2: four subset tests **NOT DETECTED**. One, capacity runs (C-run), came out **SUPPORTED** by the rule: 119 of 408
lines matched against 105.5 expected, p = 0.005. Before writing a word about scribes I ran the control that should
have been the primary from the start: move each line by one unit of its own smallest sign and count again. If the lines
were sums, the exact values would win. They lose: 142 shifted matches against 119 exact, worse than the null contrast
(p = 0.91), and the same holds in all eight tests. What the statistic picked up is likeness: a reverse line is written in
the same units and range as its own obverse. That's real but ordinary. It isn't arithmetic, and no summation claim
is made.

The PE001 audit closes cleanly: its own design reaches power 0.58 at a 10 % plant (published 0.61) and 1.00 at 20 %. The
first trench missed its gate only because of the plant size I chose.
