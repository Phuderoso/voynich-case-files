# Expedition 3 · Proto-Elamite accounts (Susa, c. 3100–2900 BCE)

> *Caderno de campo.* Fifteen hundred clay tablets from Susa, written in a script nobody can read aloud. But the
> scribes counted, and counts can be checked. We start where the clay is least ambiguous: the numbers.

A parallel dig to the Voynich trench and the khipu camp: exact, test-first analysis of the proto-Elamite corpus
transliterated by the [Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative](https://cdli.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/) (CDLI). Why this
enigma: next in line in [TRIAGE.md](../TRIAGE.md) (score 7, ahead of Linear A at 6): public machine-readable
transliterations, numerical systems with known factor tables, and totals that either add up or do not.

Method is the same as the main repo: pre-register hypothesis, metric and decision rule; calibrate every null;
plant controls; correct for multiple comparisons; say "not testable" when power is low; publish negatives.
**No decipherment is claimed.** The language of proto-Elamite is unknown; this expedition tests arithmetic and
structure only.

## Open investigative lines
1. **Exact-vs-shifted totals — closed by PE003**: single reverse totals equal the sum of the whole obverse (C-full 15 vs 0.5, p ≤ 0.0001), a known convention now reproduced with a calibrated, confound-planted test; multi-line reverses show no full or partial sums. Remaining: S/D/B-full need more tablets (power 0.45–0.61 at 35–40 lines).
2. **Damaged numerals as constraint solving — PE004 NOT VALIDATED, PE007 NOT TESTABLE**: exact when the tablet balances (85/85; PE007 68/68, 28/28), but blind to balance it is not precise enough, and dropping grain tablets does not rescue it out of sample (fresh non-CAP entry precision 0.60, CP95 high 0.72; the frequency guess wins 50 vs 28). Parked: reopens only with a much larger calibration set or a baseline-aware target (slots whose notation does not repeat on the tablet), pre-registered as its own cycle.
3. **System disambiguation by balance — PE011 NOT DETECTED.** On the 46 complete single-total tablets with all-ambiguous entries, the sum closes in one 2-way class on 19 (C 10, I 9; one single system on 10, all C — the same order as Born et al.'s 24 summary texts). Agreement of that class with the counted-object prediction is 12/17 vs 9.0 by chance (p 0.148): not shown, not ruled out (CP95 upper 0.88). The corpus has no more complete ambiguous single-total tablets; the line reopens only with multi-line reverses as summaries (Born's wider filter, pre-registered with its own null) or expert labels as an outside test set.
4. **Ratio accounts — PE014 NOT DETECTED** (powered). PE009: on non-balancing grain tablets, exact C-unit ratios reverse : obverse sum NOT DETECTED. PE014: the corpus almost never writes two notations inside one line (1 intact case of 35), so pairs are consecutive entries; on 272 informative adjacent pairs exact small ratios (p/q ≤ 6) occur 40 times vs 37.6 under a structure-preserving shuffle (p 0.343): a convention on ~10 % of pairs is EXCLUDED (power 0.97), a weak one (~5 %) is not. Within-tablet order: 40 vs 34.3 (p 0.074, Holm 0.148), a lead only. Ratio accounts are local conventions on named tablets (Born's 2.5:1 texts, P008212), not a corpus-wide habit we can see. The line reopens only with new tablets (a held-out test of the within-tablet lead and of PE013's M009 → M157 lead) or a specialist list of ration texts as an outside test set.
5. **Header structure — PE012 NOT DETECTED** (both tests powered for a strong rule: 0.88 / 1.00 at plant 0.5). The first header sign (variant dropped) predicts neither the entries' system (C / I / MIX; G p 0.404) nor their size order beyond the system (p 0.392) on 234 / 493 tablets; M157, the commonest header, heads grain and count accounts alike (C 94 / I 28 / MIX 7). Not ruled out: a moderate rule (plant 0.3 power 0.61 / 0.76). Left open: **5b** a finer key (the full header line, variant suffixes kept); PE012's descriptive tables are now seen, so 5b must disclose that and carry its own gates. **5c subscripts — PE013**: the closing line does not repeat the header (3/81 vs 7/3 expected, p 0.43; a ≥ 20 % repeat rule EXCLUDED at power 0.975); whether it predicts system or size is NOT TESTABLE on this corpus (16 system-labelled tablets; power 0.43 on 54). Post hoc lead only: subscript M009 sits under M157 on 13/13. **5b — PE015 NOT DETECTED / NOT TESTABLE**: the full header (variants and complex graphemes kept) predicts neither the system (H1G p 0.133) nor, inside its coarse key, the size beyond the system (H2W p 0.336), powered for a strong rule on two batches; system refinement (H1W, power 0.48) and global size (H2G, FPR 0.125) not testable. M157 and M157~a head the same mix (C 0.72 / 0.68). **Line 5 is closed within reach of this corpus**; it reopens only with new tablets (held-out tests of the small sharp keys M387, M136~q, M005~a, and of PE013's M009 → M157) or an expert header classification as an outside label.
6. **Balance predictor — PE005 NOT TESTABLE** (all 5 features; 43 tablets split in halves cannot confirm a strong planted predictor). Lead: capacity (grain) tablets balance 4/19 vs 18/24 for the rest (full p 0.00067; even half p 0.167), and the mismatch is not mainly the sub-unit factors (12/15 still fail on N01 and above).
7. **Capacity split — PE006 SUPPORTED out of sample**: on 64 fresh tablets grain (CAP) tablets balance 7/32 vs 18/32 (p 0.0049; gates pass), also without editor restorations (p 0.021) and within editions (p 0.0036). Mismatches are mostly total < sum (20/25), never fixed by the sub-unit factors (0/25), and long grain lists never balance (0/12).
8. **Restoration on non-capacity tablets — PE007 NOT TESTABLE** (power 0.645 / 0.565 < 0.80: even with 90 % planted balance the rule beats the frequency guess too rarely at these sizes). Descriptive: in-sample precision 0.85, fresh 0.60. The capacity split is real (PE006) but is not a sufficient balance predictor: fresh non-CAP tablets balance only 12/26.
9. **How grain totals are built — closed within reach of this corpus (PE008–PE010).** Prefix totals, "non-grain lines" totals and the upper-factor rival are EXCLUDED (PE008, ≤ 11–22 %); head count × ration and count of lines are EXCLUDED, exact C-unit ratios NOT DETECTED (PE009); one entry, the largest entry and the mean entry are EXCLUDED on fresh crates (PE010, 2/60 at chance, 0/17, 0/17; ≤ 10–16 %). The reverse number is itself grain, of the size of one entry but scattered over two orders of magnitude around it. The fresh grain crates are now spent (remaining CAP tablets have partial or broken obverses); the line reopens only with new published tablets or a new, outside-sourced convention (e.g. a specialist's reading of the grain reverse), pre-registered as its own cycle.

- **Established (control-beating, reproducible):** **PE006** grain (capacity) tablets balance less often, out of sample
  (7/32 vs 18/32, p 0.0049; also without editor restorations p 0.021 and within editions p 0.0036) — novelty to the field
  not claimed. **PE003** reproduces the known convention that a single reverse total sums the whole obverse (C-full 15 vs

  rival (PE008), head count × ration and count of lines (PE009), one / largest / mean entry (PE010); a ≥ 20 % rule that the
  closing subscript repeats the header (PE013, power 0.975); a corpus-wide exact small-ratio convention on ~10 % of
  consecutive entry pairs (PE014, power 0.97).
- **Not detected (powered for a strong effect only):** multi-line reverse subsets (PE002), C-unit ratios (PE009), sum
  closure vs counted object (PE011, 12/17 vs 9.0), header sign → system / size (PE012), full header → system and size
  beyond the coarse key (PE015).

  predictor on 43 tablets (PE005), PE001's sums, subscript → system/size (PE013), ratios inside one line (PE014, 1 intact

  (PE013), small sharp header keys (PE015: M387 C 6/6, M136~q C 5/5). All need tablets the corpus does not have.
- **No decipherment, no restoration list, no outreach.** The camp's open lines now all need new tablets or an outside
