# Expedition 3 · Proto-Elamite accounts (Susa, c. 3100–2900 BCE)

> *Caderno de campo.* Fifteen hundred clay tablets from Susa, written in a script nobody can read aloud. But the
> scribes counted, and counts can be checked. We start where the clay is least ambiguous: the numbers.

A parallel dig to the Voynich trench and the khipu camp: exact, test-first analysis of the proto-Elamite corpus
transliterated by the [Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative](https://cdli.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/) (CDLI). Why this
enigma: next in line in [TRIAGE.md](../TRIAGE.md) (score 7, ahead of Linear A at 6): public machine-readable
transliterations, numerical systems with known factor tables, and totals that either add up or do not.

Method is the same as the main repo: pre-register hypothesis, metric and decision rule; calibrate every null;
plant controls; correct for multiple comparisons; say "not testable" when power is low; publish negatives.
**No decipherment is claimed.** The language of proto-Elamite is unknown; this expedition tests arithmetic and
structure only.

## Open investigative lines
1. **Exact-vs-shifted totals — closed by PE003**: single reverse totals equal the sum of the whole obverse (C-full 15 vs 0.5, p ≤ 0.0001), a known convention now reproduced with a calibrated, confound-planted test; multi-line reverses show no full or partial sums. Remaining: S/D/B-full need more tablets (power 0.45–0.61 at 35–40 lines).
2. **Damaged numerals as constraint solving — PE004 NOT VALIDATED, PE007 NOT TESTABLE**: exact when the tablet balances (85/85; PE007 68/68, 28/28), but blind to balance it is not precise enough, and dropping grain tablets does not rescue it out of sample (fresh non-CAP entry precision 0.60, CP95 high 0.72; the frequency guess wins 50 vs 28). Parked: reopens only with a much larger calibration set or a baseline-aware target (slots whose notation does not repeat on the tablet), pre-registered as its own cycle.
3. **System disambiguation by balance**: N01/N14 notations are ambiguous across S/D/B/C; count how often the balance picks one system uniquely, and compare with the commodity sign heading the entries (Born et al. 2023 as baseline).
4. **Ratio accounts** (grain per worker, per animal, per field): exact rational ratios between paired notations (Englund's 2½ N39b per M54 example), searched with a calibrated shuffle null. PE009: on non-balancing grain tablets, exact C-unit ratios reverse : obverse sum are NOT DETECTED (1/15 clean, 1/25 exposed; chance 0.57) — ratio accounts should be sought between paired notations inside a line, not between reverse and obverse sum.
5. **Header and subscript structure**: does the sign in the header line predict the system and size of the entries? Permutation null over tablets.
6. **Balance predictor — PE005 NOT TESTABLE** (all 5 features; 43 tablets split in halves cannot confirm a strong planted predictor). Lead: capacity (grain) tablets balance 4/19 vs 18/24 for the rest (full p 0.00067; even half p 0.167), and the mismatch is not mainly the sub-unit factors (12/15 still fail on N01 and above).
7. **Capacity split — PE006 SUPPORTED out of sample**: on 64 fresh tablets grain (CAP) tablets balance 7/32 vs 18/32 (p 0.0049; gates pass), also without editor restorations (p 0.021) and within editions (p 0.0036). Mismatches are mostly total < sum (20/25), never fixed by the sub-unit factors (0/25), and long grain lists never balance (0/12).
8. **Restoration on non-capacity tablets — PE007 NOT TESTABLE** (power 0.645 / 0.565 < 0.80: even with 90 % planted balance the rule beats the frequency guess too rarely at these sizes). Descriptive: in-sample precision 0.85, fresh 0.60. The capacity split is real (PE006) but is not a sufficient balance predictor: fresh non-CAP tablets balance only 12/26.
9. **How grain totals are built — closed within reach of this corpus (PE008–PE010).** Prefix totals, "non-grain lines" totals and the upper-factor rival are EXCLUDED (PE008, ≤ 11–22 %); head count × ration and count of lines are EXCLUDED, exact C-unit ratios NOT DETECTED (PE009); one entry, the largest entry and the mean entry are EXCLUDED on fresh crates (PE010, 2/60 at chance, 0/17, 0/17; ≤ 10–16 %). The reverse number is itself grain, of the size of one entry but scattered over two orders of magnitude around it. The fresh grain crates are now spent (remaining CAP tablets have partial or broken obverses); the line reopens only with new published tablets or a new, outside-sourced convention (e.g. a specialist's reading of the grain reverse), pre-registered as its own cycle.

## Where the trench stands (after PE010)

tablets balance less often, out of sample) and one that reproduces a known convention (PE003: single reverse totals
