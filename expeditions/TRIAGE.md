# Expedition triage: which second enigma?

*Caderno de campo, 4 Nov 2026.* Before picking a second dig site next to the Voynich trench, I scored a handful of
undeciphered or poorly understood record systems on four questions. Scores are 0–3 and are my judgement, written
down before any analysis ran.

| Enigma | Public machine-readable data | Exact checks available | Active open work to contribute to | Chance of a solid, verifiable result | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inka khipu** | 3: [Open Khipu Repository](https://github.com/khipulab/open-khipu-repository) ships a SQLite database (`data/khipu.db`, 619 khipu records, about 54,000 cords and 110,000 knots) | 3: decimal knot values, sum cords, pendant/subsidiary trees, cross-khipu matches are all plain arithmetic | 3: OKR is on GitHub and maintained | 3: arithmetic relations are either there or not | **12** |
| Linear A | 2: corpora exist (e.g. [SigLA](https://sigla.phis.me/)), but not one canonical open dataset | 2: tablet totals (KU-RO lines) can be checked; little else is exact | 1 | 1: the language is unknown, so most claims stay interpretive | 6 |
| Proto-Elamite | 2: tablets transliterated in [CDLI](https://cdli.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/) | 2: numeric systems and totals | 1 | 2 | 7 |
| Indus script | 1: concordances exist but are not openly licensed in machine-readable form | 1: short seals, no totals | 1 | 0: whether it is writing at all is still debated | 3 |
| Rongorongo | 1: a few dozen objects; transcriptions scattered | 1: parallel-passage alignment only | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| Phaistos disc | 1: one object, a few hundred signs | 0: too short for statistics | 0 | 0 | 1 |

**Pick: Inka khipu.** It wins clearly: open data, exact arithmetic tests, and a live repository to give back to.
The others stay on the bench as possible later expeditions.

**Second pick (8 Nov 2026): Proto-Elamite** (score 7, next after khipu). The khipu camp's R5-mask debt is closed
and the Open Khipu Repository has no commit newer than `4039ca5`, so a new trench opens:
[expeditions/proto-elamite](proto-elamite/README.md). Linear A (6) stays next on the bench.

**Fourth pick (9 Nov 2026): Rongorongo** (score 4, next after Linear A). Linear A's open lines closed on machine-readable data after LA003, and the Open Khipu Repository is still at `4039ca5`. New trench: [expeditions/rongorongo](rongorongo/README.md). A careful scripted study already exists (rochal/rongorongo), so the trench adds calibration (nulls and power), not rediscovery.
