## Why
Two earlier findings pull against each other. C083/C085: Timm–Schinner generator settings that keep the manuscript's surface statistics (word length, type/token ratio TTR, glyph-bigram entropy h2, position rigidity R; C004/C005) exist — all with **random** source choice — and match its first/last-symbol coupling. C084: the manuscript's pages carry topics (a page model fitted on odd lines predicts even lines; gain X in bits/token above a line-reassignment null), which the surface-matched C005 setting **loses** (X ≤ 0.10/0.15) while the **default** setting (same-page copying) keeps (0.32–0.62) but fails the surface (TTR 0.215 vs 0.354). **Question: within a grid fixed in advance, does any setting satisfy both, on held-out seeds?** C084 was the last new family; C085 and C086 are follow-ups, so **C087 must open a new approach family**.

## Design step (before the push; `tools/c086_design.py`, `analysis/C086_design.json/.txt`; non-registered or spent seeds)
- **The manuscript's own X has an init spread.** Re-measuring the C084 statistic with 8 different PLSA initialisations (R = 9, seeds 9086xx) gives ZL W1 0.460 ± 0.039 (0.387–0.508), IT2a W1 0.467 ± 0.013, ZL W2 0.221 ± 0.042, IT2a W2 0.217 ± 0.035. The C084 single-init W1 values (0.546/0.538) sit **above all 16 repeats**: the PLSA fit G of the observed pages varies with the start (G from −0.08 to −0.27 bits on ZL W1 across 17 inits; C084's init gave −0.114), and C084's `null_sd` does not include that spread. → **C084 audit note** (readings unchanged; values re-quoted as multi-init means). The topic band below therefore uses multi-init means.
- **Topic band** (per window, = [0.85 × min, 1.15 × max] of the two transcriptions' multi-init means, mirroring the ±15% surface rule): **W1 [0.3911, 0.5372], W2 [0.1841, 0.2542]**.
- **I-119 rates** (resampling / exchangeable draws): a manuscript-like cell passes the training band in both windows (3-run mean) with probability ≈ 0.91; the K2 positive control passes ≈ 0.94; on the 30 spent C005 runs from C084, an exchangeable run lies INSIDE the range of 20 others with p ≈ 0.91 (W1) / 0.90 (W2) and is wrongly called ABOVE/BELOW with p ≤ 0.002, so the G3 held-out gate passes with probability ≈ 1.00 and the REACHED branch, if the truth is "equivalent", is reached with probability ≈ 0.8 (both windows INSIDE). No gate fails by construction.
- **Smoke** (reserved seed 11999, 6 cells): page/position cells at default morphing gave TTR 0.22–0.27 (infeasible); C005 cell surface-feasible with X 0.01/0.05; default X 0.67/0.65 (overshooting the band). **Disclosure:** the smoke also ran the K2 positive control (IT2a manuscript, its registered seeds), giving X 0.450/0.250 — inside the band; nothing was changed after seeing it.
- **Grid widened before the push** (no registered seed touched): a first 64-cell draft lacked the suggestion switch; `method.suggestions = top` inserts the most frequent types and lowers TTR, so `suggestions {top, none}` was added as a mechanistic knob (128 cells). The jar has no "probability of same-page copying" switch; its topic-capable choosers are `page` and `position`.

## Pre-registration (pushed before the run; tool `tools/c086_joint.py`, analysis seed 8686)
- **Data:** ZL 3b (`data/ZL3b-n.txt`, IVTFF "Version 3b of 13/05/2025", md5 1b9c653b4194b6841cb2f010558de1c8) and IT2a (`data/IT2a-n.txt`, "Version 2a of 02/02/2023 modified 25/06/2025", md5 0e7b3a6a3053c7d35a52feed8ff103de). Windows as C084: W1 f1r–f79v (131 pages, 15,186 tokens), W2 f86v6–f116r (57 pages, 15,011 tokens).
- **Grid (130 cells, fixed):** sourceChooser {page, position} × add_remove {20, 60} × replace {50, 100} × combine_split {30, 0} × canFollow {curveline, none} × suggestions {top, none} × lines_per_page {29, 12}, other settings default (cell 0 = all defaults); cell 128 = C005 selected configuration; cell 129 = defaults (control duplicate of cell 0). 3,600 lines per run.
- **Seeds (fresh):** training **11391–11393** (all cells), reference **11394–11413**, held-out **11414–11423**, boundary re-runs **11424–11443**. Next free: **11444**.
- **Surface feasibility:** 3-run mean of mwl, TTR, h2, R (first 6,000 words) within ±15% of 4.566 / 0.354 / 2.249 / 0.487.
- **Topic X** (C084 statistic: PLSA K 8, 150 iterations, odd → even lines, ε 0.1, line-reassignment null, R 9 in training, R 19 in confirmation) on pseudo-pages cut to the ZL page sizes of W1 and W2, **only for surface-feasible cells plus the two control cells** (compute budget; disclosed). **Topic feasible:** 3-run mean X inside the band in both windows. **Jointly feasible:** both.
- **Selection:** jointly feasible cell with the smallest worst relative deviation over the 6 numbers (4 surface, 2 topic); ties → lower index.
- **Confirmation (single test):** selected cell on 20 reference + 10 held-out seeds (R 19). Manuscript (multi-init mean, init SD as sd) per window and transcription: ABOVE iff X − 2sd > max(ref), BELOW iff X + 2sd < min(ref), INSIDE iff in [min, max], else NOT CALLED.
- **H1 branches:**
  - no surface-feasible grid cell → **NOT TESTABLE** (the grid cannot keep the surface);
  - surface-feasible cells but none jointly feasible → **NOT REACHED IN THIS GRID**, with two-sided grid extremes per window (min/max training X over surface-feasible cells: **UNDERSHOOT** if all below the band, **OVERSHOOT** if all above, **BRACKETED** otherwise) and boundary cells B1 (surface-feasible, closest in topics) and B2 (topic-feasible, closest in surface, if any) each re-run on 10 fresh seeds → infeasibility **CONFIRMED / NOT CONFIRMED**;
  - jointly feasible → confirmation: 20-run means still jointly feasible and all 4 manuscript calls INSIDE → **REACHED JOINTLY**; means no longer feasible → **NOT CONFIRMED ON HELD-OUT**; any ABOVE/BELOW → **NOT REACHED** (ABOVE = manuscript beyond the cell, BELOW = cell overshoots); else **NOT CALLED**.
- **Gates (fail → NOT TESTABLE):** **K1** known answers on the new seeds: cell 128 surface-feasible and below the W1 band; cell 129 surface-infeasible. **K2** positive control: IT2a manuscript text through the same pipeline (surface on its first 6,000 words; X = mean of 3 inits on its real pages) jointly feasible. **F** FPR: 5 line-reassigned generator texts, p ≤ 0.05 in ≤ 1/5 per window. Confirmation only: **G3** ≤ 2/10 held-out runs called ABOVE/BELOW per window; **G4** ≥ 18/20 reference runs usable.
- **What a result can mean:** REACHED JOINTLY = one generator setting reproduces both properties at this resolution (not a mechanism, says nothing about meaning). NOT REACHED IN THIS GRID = not excluded for the generator family (other knobs exist), only for these 128 settings.

## Results (run 07:13–07:16 PT, 164 s, seed 8686; `analysis/C086_results.json`, `analysis/C086_run_log.txt`)
**H1: NOT TESTABLE — no surface-feasible cell in the grid.** None of the 128 page/position cells kept all four surface statistics within ±15% on the training seeds 11391–11393, so the joint question could not be posed inside this grid. This is not an exclusion of the generator family: it says only that these 128 settings with topic-capable source choice cannot hold the surface.

- **Gates all pass.** K1: cell 128 (C005) surface-feasible (mwl 5.05, TTR 0.365, h2 2.47, R 0.514) with X 0.034 (W1) / 0.059 (W2), below the band; cell 129 (default) surface-infeasible (TTR 0.225). K2: IT2a manuscript text jointly feasible (surface mwl 4.37, TTR 0.375, h2 2.34, R 0.453; X 0.450 / 0.250, inside both bands — the W2 value is 0.004 below the band's top). F: line-reassigned generator texts p ≤ 0.05 in 1/5 (W1) and 0/5 (W2).
- **Where the surface fails (128 cells, all usable):** TTR alone 56 cells; R alone 26; TTR + R 28; mwl + TTR 12; mwl + TTR + R 6. Grid ranges: TTR 0.122–0.419, R 0.172–0.597, mwl 3.57–4.92, h2 1.94–2.56. 30 cells reach TTR ≥ 0.30 (the band's floor) but every one of them loses positional rigidity R (closest: cell 102, position chooser, add_remove 60, canFollow none, no suggestions: TTR 0.333, R 0.249 vs 0.487). The closest cell overall (cell 3, page chooser, no suggestions, 12-line pages) misses by 26% (TTR 0.261, R 0.398).
- **Grid-extreme reporting (I-107/I-117):** not applicable to X (no surface-feasible cell, so no training X computed by rule). On the surface side the extreme is the smallest worst-deviation, 0.262 (cell 3), versus the ±0.15 bar.
- **A knob that does nothing (disclosed):** `method.morph.replace.probability` 50 vs 100 gave byte-identical statistics in all 64 pairs, so the grid has 64 distinct settings, not 128. Doesn't change the verdict.
- **Branches not reached:** joint-feasible selection, held-out confirmation (seeds 11394–11423) and boundary re-runs (11424–11443) were not run; those seeds are **reserved but unused**. To keep the bookkeeping simple they're treated as spent. Next free seed: **11444**.

### Exploratory (after seeing the surface table; no confirmatory weight; `tools/c086_exploratory.py`, `analysis/C086_exploratory.json`)
Topic X (R 9) on the training seeds for three near-miss cells picked after the fact: cell 3 (page, closest surface) **0.505 / 0.543**; cell 102 (position, closest TTR-feasible) **0.420 / 0.525**; cell 39 (page, TTR-feasible) **0.823 / 0.890** (W1 / W2; band W1 0.391–0.537, W2 0.184–0.254). The default control: 0.448 / 0.532. **Lead:** in every topic-capable setting tried, the generator's topic strength is about the same in both windows or higher in W2 (0.43–0.89), whereas the manuscript's falls by half from W1 (≈ 0.46) to W2 (≈ 0.22). W1 is reachable (four of five values in or above the band); W2 is overshot by every topic-capable setting. So the C084 tension has two parts: (a) under page/position copying, raising vocabulary variety destroys positional rigidity, and (b) the generator's page topics are uniform across the book while the manuscript's vary by section. Both are hypotheses for a future pre-registered test, not results.
