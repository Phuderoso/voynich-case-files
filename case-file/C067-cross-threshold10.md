**Question:** C066 found that every section-diagnostic word type has a one-glyph neighbour with ≥ 2 tokens in another section (ceiling, D = 0). Its **post-hoc** look at a stricter threshold (neighbour with **≥ 10 tokens outside the home section**) suggested that the manuscript's section-diagnostic words are *more* tied to other sections than the Timm–Schinner generator's (O(diagnostic) 0.95–0.96 vs 0.75–0.90 in 10 runs) and far more than natural topic words (0.13–0.78). **This hypothesis was suggested by C066 data.** C067 fixes the threshold in advance and tests it on fresh generator seeds, with a type-level null that a plant cannot be absorbed into. README check (2026-11-08): no earlier cycle tested this threshold or this null; row 66 and the C067 backlog list it as open.

**Honesty about freshness:** the manuscript values at this threshold (S10 0.962 ZL / 0.948 IT2a, D10 −0.034 / −0.051) and the natural-text range were already printed in C066's post-hoc file. Only the generator seeds (8201–8330) and the D10 permutation p are new. A confirmation therefore rests on the generator comparison.

## Pre-registration (pushed before running the C067 tool)
- **Data:** `data/ZL3b-n.txt` (ZL IVTFF 3b of 13/05/2025) and `data/IT2a-n.txt` (Takahashi IT 2a of 02/02/2023, mod. 25/06/2025), sha256 in `data/MANIFEST.md`. Corpus, glyph units, f ≥ 4, home section, binomial diagnostic rule (p < 0.001) and matching strata exactly as C066 (`tools/c066_cross_section_neighbours.py`, imported unchanged).
- **Fixed threshold:** O10(w) = 1 if another type at glyph-unit Levenshtein distance 1 has **≥ 10 tokens outside h(w)**. **S10** = share of diagnostic types with O10 = 1. **D10** = stratum-weighted O10(diagnostic) − O10(matched non-diagnostic), C066 strata and weights.
- **T1/T2 (primary): S10 vs generator.** 40 default Timm–Schinner runs (4,200 lines), **fresh seeds 8201–8260** (first 40 long enough), each cut to the transcription's token count, 4 consecutive pseudo-sections with the manuscript's section shares. Two-sided rank p around the reference median (floor 1/41 = 0.0244). **Gates** on 20 held-out runs, **fresh seeds 8301–8330** (first 20 long enough): FPR — p ≤ 0.05 in ≤ 2/20; power — **connection plant** (the direction the lead predicts): for randomly chosen diagnostic types with O10 = 0, up to 0.12 × n_diag of them, 10 random tokens outside the home section are replaced by w + marker glyph (a new one-glyph neighbour with 10 outside tokens); success = p ≤ 0.05 and above the median, in **≥ 16/20**. Size 0.12 = the gap the lead claims (≈ 0.955 − 0.835). The 15% isolation plant (C066) is reported as a downward power check, not a gate.
- **T3/T4 (primary): D10 vs matched types.** 2,000 permutations of the diagnostic label among types **within** C066 strata (home section × frequency bin × length bin); two-sided p around the null mean. This null permutes *type* labels, not page labels, so a plant on diagnostic types moves the observed D10 but not the null (the C066 HB flaw was page relabelling within Currier × hand, which barely moves pages because section ≈ stratum). **Gates** (on ZL, applied to both): FPR — 20 random pseudo-diagnostic sets with the same per-stratum counts, each vs 500 permutations, p ≤ 0.05 in ≤ 2/20; power — 20 isolation plants (15% of true diagnostic types), each vs 500 permutations, p ≤ 0.05 and below the null mean in ≥ 16/20. Gate results are computed and printed before any manuscript statistic in the run.
- **Multiple comparisons:** Benjamini–Hochberg q = 0.05 over the primary tests that pass their gates (≤ 4).
- **Verdicts:** gate fails → **NOT TESTABLE** (not excluded); gated + BH-rejected → **ABOVE / BELOW**; gated, not rejected → **NO DIFFERENCE SHOWN** (powered only against the plant size). The lead is **CONFIRMED** only if T1 and T2 are both ABOVE; **NOT CONFIRMED** if both gated and not rejected (or rejected BELOW); otherwise PARTIAL / NOT TESTABLE.
- **Secondary, descriptive (no correction):** (i) natural texts with ≥ 29,000 tokens (C058 set: Dante, Machiavelli, Cervantes, Kalevala, Kivi, Dante and Cervantes devowelled = 7–8 texts), cut to min(len, manuscript N), same split; LOO outside-range ≤ 3 → INSIDE / OUTSIDE-side (not fresh: seen in C066 post-hoc). (ii) thresholds 5 and 20 for manuscript and generator reference. (iii) D10 vs the generator range.
