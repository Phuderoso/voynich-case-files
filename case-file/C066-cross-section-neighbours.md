**Why this question (and why not the obvious one):** the obvious F-CROSS question — "is vocabulary shared between sections more or less than expected given hand and Currier language?" — is already answered here: C001/C002 showed with a calibrated null that section carries vocabulary information beyond hand × Currier language, and Sterneck, Polish & Bowern (arXiv 2107.02858) and Montemurro & Zanette (PLoS ONE 2013) show topics/keywords align with sections. So C066 asks a different, sharper question about *how* sections differ. Timm & Schinner (Cryptologia 44:1, 2019; Cryptologia 2026, doi 10.1080/01611194.2026.2693462) argue that all word types form one network of similar words and that section vocabularies evolve by small changes from one another (e.g. *chol/chor → cheol → chey → chedy*). In natural multi-topic text, the words that mark a topic are mostly content words (names, technical nouns), which need not be one-letter variants of the rest of the text's vocabulary. So: **are the manuscript's section-diagnostic word types spelling variants of words used in other sections, more or less than matched non-diagnostic types — and how does that compare with the self-citation generator and with natural texts?** As far as we found, the single-network claim has not been tested in this matched, calibrated form (frequency × length × home section matching, page-permutation null, planted gates). Section-level word overlap was looked at here only for labels (C015–C017) and for leaves/bifolia (F-COD, C021–C023), not for running-text section-diagnostic words.

## Pre-registration (pushed before any C066 number on the manuscript)
- **Data:** `data/ZL3b-n.txt` (ZL IVTFF 3b of 13/05/2025) and `data/IT2a-n.txt` (Takahashi IT 2a of 02/02/2023, mod. 25/06/2025), sha256 in `data/MANIFEST.md`. Paragraph text with the C047 parser, glyph units as C003. Pages with `$I` ∈ {H herbal, B balneo/biological, P pharma, S stars}; A/C/T/Z pages and pages without paragraph text are left out (zodiac has no `P` loci). Feasibility counts only (no statistic): ZL ≈ 30.8k tokens (H 11.3k, B 6.2k, P 2.1k, S 11.2k), IT2a similar. Currier × hand strata with more than one of these sections: A1 (H 95 pages, P 15), B2 (H 20, B 19), B3 (H 6, S 22); the others are fixed under HB.
- **Statistic D:** per type w with frequency f ≥ 4: home section h(w) = the section with most tokens of w; **diagnostic** if the exact binomial upper tail P(X ≥ count in h | f, token share of h) < 0.001. O(w) = 1 if another type at glyph-unit Levenshtein distance 1 has ≥ 2 tokens outside h(w). D = stratum-weighted mean of [mean O(diagnostic) − mean O(non-diagnostic)] over strata (home section × frequency bin {4–5, 6–9, 10–19, 20–49, ≥ 50} × length bin {≤ 3, 4, 5, 6, ≥ 7 units}), weight = number of diagnostic types; strata without both groups are skipped. D < 0: section-diagnostic words are more isolated than matched words; D > 0: more tied by one-glyph variants to other sections.
- **HA (primary, generator-referenced):** Voynich D (ZL, IT2a) vs 40 runs of the default Timm–Schinner generator (4,200 lines, **fresh seeds 7701–7760**, first 40 with enough tokens), each cut to the manuscript's token count and split into 4 consecutive pseudo-sections with the manuscript's section token shares in manuscript order (H, B, P, S). Two-sided rank p, floor 1/41 = 0.0244. **Gates** (20 held-out runs, seeds 7801–7830): FPR — unplanted runs with p ≤ 0.05 must be ≤ 2/20; power — **isolation plant** (15% of that text's diagnostic types, all their tokens, get a unique marker glyph so they have no neighbours) detected (p ≤ 0.05, below the median) in ≥ 16/20. Feasibility only (seed 7699, not used): one generator run gives ~38k tokens, ~96 diagnostic types, O near ceiling (0.95–0.97); the 15% plant moves D by about −0.12.
- **HB (primary, structure-preserving null):** Voynich true-section D vs 1,000 relabellings that permute section labels among pages **within Currier language × scribal hand** (`$L` × `$H`) strata. Two-sided p around the null mean. **Gates** (measured on ZL, applied to both): FPR — 20 relabelled texts treated as truth, each vs 200 further relabellings, p ≤ 0.05 in ≤ 2/20; power — 20 isolation plants (15%) on the true sections, each vs 200 relabellings of the planted text, p ≤ 0.05 and below the null mean in ≥ 16/20.
- **Multiple comparisons:** Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.05 over the 4 primary tests (HA × 2, HB × 2) that pass their gates.
- **Verdicts per test:** BH-rejected → **ABOVE / BELOW** (generator or null); gated but not rejected → **NO DIFFERENCE SHOWN** (powered against a 15% isolation shift only); gate fails → **NOT TESTABLE** (not excluded).
- **Secondary, descriptive (no correction):** (i) 12 natural texts (C058 set: Latin, Italian, Spanish, Hebrew, Finnish, 3 devowelled), each cut to min(own length, manuscript token count) — texts < 16,000 tokens dropped; 6 of 12 are shorter than the manuscript, so N differs (disclosed) — same 4-block split; LOO gate (outside-range ≤ 3 of n), then INSIDE / OUTSIDE-side. (ii) Exact within-stratum label-permutation p for D ≠ 0 (2,000 permutations) and raw O rates.
- **Reading rules:** D is about spelling neighbourhoods of word forms, not about meaning. Generator and natural "sections" are consecutive blocks (drift / chapter shifts), the manuscript's are illustration sections in bound order — a disclosed asymmetry. ZL and IT2a share pages: two readings, not two samples. Contiguous blocks vs real sections, and O near ceiling, can both limit what D shows.
- **Tool:** `tools/c066_cross_section_neighbours.py` → `analysis/C066_results.json`; seed 6666; `timeout 600`; 8 processes. Tool smoke-tested on one natural text and one generator run (seed 7699) only, before this commit.
